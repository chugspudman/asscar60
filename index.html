Rules and Information

Intro

The world of ASSCAR we all know and love, but like fantasy football. I imagine the other ASSCAR league is still going on in the world, and ASSCAR60 is another form of the sport altogether. Like NASCAR and F1.

Overall Structure

Each season is 1 month long of real-time. There are 20 races in a season, one per weekday, and they occur at 8PM eastern. Qualifiers take place 10 minutes before the race. Each race is 60 laps.

Race 1 of a season does not occur until the Monday after the opening draft is completed. After that, races continue on the weekday schedule.

Each manager controls 1 team. Each team fields two cars per race. The races are relays, and each car must have 3 drivers across the course of the race. Drivers cannot switch cars during a race, but their car assignment and relay position can be changed outside of the race.

The driver who begins the race is the Opener, the one who finishes the race is the Closer, and the Bridge is between them. The minimum number of laps allowed for a driver's stint is 5, and the maximum is 40, meaning different drivers can be racing for different lengths of time as determined by the manager.

Relay plans may be edited before qualifiers begin. Once qualifiers begin, relay plans are locked until after that race.

Managers will manage their team by drafting drivers, selecting a Pit Coach specialty, setting relay plans, completing trades with other managers, voting on league events, and selecting weekly driver trainings and car upgrades.

Drafts

There is an opening draft before each season, followed by a vote for the Initiation Martyr for that season. Then, after Race 10, there is a rookie draft.

Opening Draft

Before the first season, managers draft from a generated pool of 60 drivers. The Season 1 opening draft lasts 8 rounds, with each team drafting 8 drivers.

Before later seasons, each manager retains 1 veteran and 1 rookie from their previous season roster. All non-retained drivers return to the draft pool. The draft pool is then filled back up to 60 drivers, using returning drivers as much as possible. Opening drafts after Season 1 last 6 rounds, filling each team back to 8 drivers.

Opening draft order is reverse-standings snake order. Draft picks may be traded, so a team may sometimes make a pick in another team's original draft slot.

Before an opening draft can begin, all managers must complete the required pre-draft steps. For Season 1, each manager must select a Pit Coach specialty and vote to begin the draft. For later seasons, each manager must select retainers, select a Pit Coach specialty, and vote to begin the draft.

After the opening draft is complete, the remaining eligible drivers become candidates for the Initiation Martyr. Managers vote on which driver will be martyred. Whoever is chosen is added to In Memoriam and removed from the league. The remaining candidates become free agents.

Rookie Draft

After Race 10, a rookie draft class of 24 drivers is generated. These drivers generally have worse current stats but higher potential than opening draft drivers.

The rookie draft does not begin automatically. Managers vote to begin it using the league code. Once all six managers have voted, the draft begins.

The rookie draft proceeds in reverse-standings snake order for two rounds, for a total of 12 newly signed rookie drivers. Each pick has a 12-hour timer.

After the rookie draft, each team must release one already-signed non-rookie driver to free agency. A rookie drafted during the current season cannot be released to free agency during that same season.

Pit Coaches

Before each season's opening draft begins, each manager selects one Pit Coach specialty from the same list of available specialties. Once a specialty is selected, that team's Pit Coach receives a randomly generated name.

A team's Pit Coach name is visible to other managers on the Teams page. The Pit Coach specialty is private and is only visible to that manager on their Admin page.

Pit Coach specialties last for the full season unless changed by a future rule or Season Event.

Qualifiers

10 minutes before each race, qualifiers are run. Each car for each team runs 3 laps during the qualifiers. The first lap is done by the car's assigned Opener, the second lap by the assigned Bridge, and the third lap by the assigned Closer.

Mishaps and spin-outs can occur during qualifiers. Strange effects, overtakes, and stat degradation do not occur during qualifiers.

The fastest car will be put in pole position and have no time penalty assigned to their first lap. The second fastest car will be put in the 2nd position and will have a penalty of 0.2 seconds added to their first lap. Third will have a penalty of 0.4, and so on to 12th place with a penalty of 2.2 seconds.

These time penalties are only added to the cars' first laps. The results of the qualifiers are posted in the Race Feed and the Running Order is updated accordingly before the race begins.

Stats

Driver Stats

Control
Helps drivers get through turns and chicanes cleanly. Higher Control reduces the time lost through technical course features and lowers the chance of mishaps.
Overtaking
Helps a driver attack or defend during passing attempts. Higher Overtaking makes a driver more likely to complete passes, deny passes, and avoid overtake-related trouble. It is compared to the Overtaking of the opposing driver.
Stamina
Helps a driver maintain performance over the course of their stint. Higher Stamina slows how much their stats degrade while they remain in the car.
Potential
Indicates how many more stat points the driver is able to train.

Car Stats

Speed
Helps the car go faster. Higher Speed makes the car cover more distance per second.
Handling
Helps the car survive technical course sections. Higher Handling reduces time lost in turns and chicanes, lowers mishap risk, and can soften some handling-related penalties.
Durability
Helps the car hold together as the race goes on. Higher Durability slows car stat degradation over the full race, especially during longer runs.

Overtaking

During a race, cars may attempt overtakes when they are close enough to another car on the course. Overtakes can happen even between cars that are on different laps, because all cars are physically sharing the same course.

When an overtake is attempted, the chance of it succeeding is based on the difference between the two cars' Overtaking scores. If the attacker wins, the attacking car gains position. If the defender wins, the attack is denied.

A car that attempts an overtake and fails cannot attempt another overtake for a short cooldown period. Defending against an overtake does not create a cooldown for the defending car.

Overtake attempts can sometimes cause mishaps or spin-outs.

Trainings and Upgrades

Each week, every manager is given the same randomized set of 3 driver training options and the same randomized set of 3 car upgrade options.

Each team normally receives 2 weekly driver trainings and 1 weekly car upgrade. These opportunities may be traded.

Trainings are for drivers, and upgrades are for cars. Car stats do not have a cap, but each driver stat does. Driver stat caps are hidden and can only be discovered by training.

Every time a driver undergoes training, their Potential decreases by 1 unless a cap was discovered. When a stat cap is discovered it is publicly recorded on that driver's racer card with a small triangle in the top left corner of that stat tile.

The only drivers eligible for a given week's training are the ones who participated in at least 1 race that week. The same driver cannot receive both weekly trainings in the same week. The same stat may be trained on two different drivers.

Trainings and upgrades are not available before the first race of the season begins. Once available, managers can lock in their choices during the week. Locked-in choices take effect at 12:01 AM Eastern on the Sunday after that week's races. If a training or upgrade is not used during the week, it does not carry over to the following week.

While stats can temporarily degrade during a race for both drivers and cars, those degradations completely resolve between races and are not permanent.

Trades

Managers can propose trades with other managers through the Admin page. Managers can also exchange a currently-signed driver from their team in exchange for a Free Agent throughout the season.

Trades may include multiple drivers from each side. Trades may also include opening draft picks, weekly training opportunities, and weekly car upgrade opportunities.

A traded draft pick gives the receiving team the original team's pick in that round. A traded weekly training or upgrade gives the receiving team that opportunity for the relevant week.

If a driver, draft pick, training, or upgrade opportunity has already been traded away, it cannot be added to another trade offer unless it is reacquired.

Courses

There are 6 courses in the league: Race City, New Torque City, Acceleton, Suzuka, Skidsburg, and Velociudad. Each season uses 4 of them. Each has length, turns, chicanes, and chances for the types of weather conditions.

The two courses not used in a season are guaranteed to appear in the following season.

Each course has a length, a set of turns and chicanes, and weather condition chances. Length affects how long a perfect race on that course would take. Longer courses take more time to complete.

Turns and chicanes are each given a severity rating. When a car reaches a turn or chicane, it takes a small severity-based time penalty. Driver Control and car Handling reduce this penalty. The car also makes a mishap roll, with higher severity making mishaps more likely and higher Control and Handling making them less likely.

All non-turn and non-chicane parts of a course are open course space where overtakes may occur.

Weather Conditions

Sunny
No added mishap risk. This is the baseline condition.
Rain
Slightly increases mishap chance in turns and chicanes. Drivers/cars with better Control and Handling resist this better.
Snow
Moderately increases mishap chance in turns and chicanes. Again, Control and Handling help offset this.
Churn
An arcane, esoteric roiling of reality. Does not increase the general chance of mishaps or strange events. Instead, when strange effects do happen, Churn allows special, more severe Churning-only effects to occur.

Race Events, Marks, and Speed Madness

Strange race events can happen during races. These may temporarily help or harm drivers or cars.

The Mark of The Speed God is a permanent mark that can be given to a driver. Marked drivers are more likely to receive good race events than bad ones.

Speed Madness is a temporary state that maxes a driver's active stats for the rest of their stint. A driver who experiences Speed Madness must sit out the next race to recover.

A driver may also be revealed as a Compuzoid through a Season Event. Compuzoids have their pronouns changed to It/It and receive a Compuzoid symbol on their driver card.

Season Events

Season Events can occur between races. Every day of the season at 6AM Eastern, the league rolls for whether a Season Event happens.

Season Events may be flavor-only, or they may have mechanical effects. Mechanical Season Events can add or remove Marks, suspend drivers, trigger Speed Madness, reveal Compuzoids, change driver identities or stats, or cause other league effects.

When a manager logs in, any Season Events that occurred since their last login are shown in a NewsflASSh pop-up.

Season Events are also recorded on the Events page under the League tab.

Points and Championship

Team Championship

Each car is awarded points based on the position it finishes a race in. A team scores points for both of its cars.

At the end of the 20th race, teams may be offered the chance to make a Dark Sacrifice before the season champion is officially crowned.

Dark Sacrifice

After Race 20, before final championship standings are resolved, each team may choose one signed driver as a Dark Sacrifice, or choose no one.

A sacrificed driver dies, is removed from the team, cannot participate in future drafts, and is added to In Memoriam.

A team that performs a Dark Sacrifice receives +100 points to its season score before final standings are calculated.

A team that performs a Dark Sacrifice also receives The Stain of Sin for the following season. The Stain of Sin is shown on that team's card. While a team has The Stain of Sin, drivers on that team are more likely to undergo negative race events than good ones. The Stain of Sin is removed after that following season ends.

Most Valuable Driver

Each driver is assigned a proportional share of their cars' points at the end of each race based on how long they drove during it. The Opener is then given an extra 0.01 points, and the Closer is given an extra 0.02 points.

At the end of the 20th race, the league's Most Valuable Driver (MVD) is calculated by tallying each driver's points from across the season. This can sometimes result in a tie. There is no mechanical advantage to winning this.

Media

The Media page is a place for in-world articles, stories, commentary, and other pieces written by players.

Media entries are listed from newest to oldest. Clicking an entry expands it to show the full piece.

The manager who created a media entry can edit or delete it. Devman can edit or delete any media entry.

When a new media entry is posted, managers who have not yet read it will receive a notification badge.

In Memoriam

Drivers who die or are martyred are recorded in In Memoriam.

This includes Initiation Martyrs, Dark Sacrifices, and any other drivers who die through future league events. In Memoriam records each driver's name, pronouns, cause, completed career laps, and career podiums.

The Dark Sacrifice

In the dark hours of the morning after race 20, a figure appears and wakes you from sleep. Its countenance is that of a withered old man wrapped in gray robes of rotting burlap. His hem reaches to the floor and appears to be carved seamlessly from the very hardwood it rests upon. He is so still that, by comparison, the walls and ceiling around him twitch. After interminable moments of silence, his lips part and with a voice that sounds as though it were carved from granite he speaks: "Lo, the races are run, but a champion has yet to be crowned. Cast one of your own into me, and you will be rewarded. But, remember, all things have a price."
